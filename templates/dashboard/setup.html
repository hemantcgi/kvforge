Choose your addons

Edit the "addons" list in your config. Available built-in addons: indexing, inference, training, background, sync, monitoring, mcp, model_scout, multimodal, analytics.

3

Configure each addon

Fill in "addon_config" with per-addon settings. At minimum, indexing needs embed_model and vector_dim; inference needs llm_model; training needs checkpoint_dir and replay_db.

4

Restart the dashboard

Run kvforge start --config your-config.json to reload this dashboard with your configuration.
