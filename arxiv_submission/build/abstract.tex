Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) grounds language-model answers in external knowledge but keeps that knowledge outside the model's weights, re-fetching and re-encoding the same context on every query. We study \textbf{progressive parametric absorption}: fine-tuning a small language model (SLM) on a corpus until it can answer an increasing share of queries \emph{from its weights}, with retrieval retained as a fallback. \textbf{Diversity of the training signal, not model capacity, is the dominant lever.} In matched-rank 2×2 ablations on a 500-question held-out set (one corpus, single judge), adding diverse rewrites of source chunks to a fixed question--answer pool raises held-out factual accuracy by +0.030 to +0.042, versus a non-significant +0.009 from a 180× increase in question volume, and the effect transfers to a second architecture: a 3.2B Llama-3.2-3B reaches parity (0.254) with a 5.1B Gemma-4-E2B (0.249) on the identical recipe (comparison on total parameters; the Gemma models are \textasciitilde2.3B/\textasciitilde4.5B \emph{effective}). Two pre-registered, blind-judged studies sharpen the picture: among LoRA training-configuration choices, adapter \textbf{placement} (all linear layers vs.~attention-only, at matched parameter budget) is the dominant retention lever (+0.067 in a two-seed blind pilot, per-fact Wilcoxon p=0.0001; +0.123 full-scale and +0.106 on leak-removed facts in a single-seed promotion), while added exposure is marginal and cross-chunk relational structure is inert; and a RAFT-trained generator \textbf{complements} retrieval precisely where retrieval fails (difference-in-differences interaction +0.135, 95\% CI {[}+0.062, +0.206{]}).

These findings are produced on \textbf{KVForge}, a platform that advances an SLM-backed QA deployment through three phases under an automatic gating heuristic: standard RAG, per-chunk KV-cache injection, and confidence-gated parametric answering. The results above live in the parametric phase (Phase 3). The KV-injection phase (Phase 2), in its current mean-pooled form, is a \textbf{characterized negative result} consistent with the KV-reuse literature, not a validated speedup. This paper reports no head-to-head comparison against an external system under matched hardware, base model, and query set.
